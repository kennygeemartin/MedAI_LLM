What MedAI does

MedAI is a research application for primary healthcare education. It searches an administrator-reviewed health library and shows source links. When an AI service is connected, it can help explain those sources. Otherwise, you see relevant library excerpts.

Know its limits

It cannot diagnose conditions, prescribe medication, or replace a clinician. Emergency screening is a basic rule-based safeguard and may miss emergencies. Seek urgent professional help when needed, even if the chat does not flag your message.

Your information

Your name, email, conversations, and feedback are stored by the application. Authorized administrators can inspect interactions for service review. If AI is enabled, recent conversation text is sent to the configured inference service. Avoid sharing identification numbers, addresses, or other unnecessary personal details.

You can delete a conversation using the button above it. This removes its messages and feedback from the active database. Operator-managed backups may have separate retention periods. This preview has no self-service account deletion or password recovery; contact the deployment operator for help.

Reviewed sources, visible references

Draft documents are excluded from answers. An administrator must review and approve them before publication. Approval records indicate an operator review, not independent medical certification.
